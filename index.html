How to read this

  • Every price is the total for the whole return trip (there and back) for 2 adults + 1 child, on Cathay Pacific only.
  • The flights listed are the outbound flights, all in Premium Economy. The flight home is already included in the price: Google pairs each outbound flight with its cheapest flight back.
  • Airlines don't publish how many seats are left. A listed price means at least 3 seats at that price. ▲ marks a jump of 10% or more since the previous check, which usually means the cheaper seats sold out.
